## firecrawl_scrape(url: str, formats: ["markdown"], onlyMainContent: bool)
- Best for: scraping JavaScript-heavy sites and SPAs
- Use ONLY when Jina read_url fails (Firecrawl costs credits)
- Prefer formats=["markdown"] with onlyMainContent=true
- Timeout: 35s, retries: 1

Rules: Last resort for extraction. Try Jina first.
