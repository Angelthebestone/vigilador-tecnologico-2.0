## brave_web_search(query: str, count: 1-20, freshness: "pd"|"pw"|"pm"|"py")
- Best for: general web search, privacy-focused
- Max query length: 400 chars
- freshness="pd" = past day, "pw" = past week, "pm" = past month
- Timeout: 20s, retries: 2

## brave_news_search(query: str, count: 1-50, freshness: "pd"|"pw"|"pm"|"py")
- Best for: real-time news search
- Default freshness="pd" (last 24 hours)
- Timeout: 15s, retries: 2

Rules: Brave is best for real-time news. Use freshness="pd" by default.
