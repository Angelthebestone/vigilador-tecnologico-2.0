## search_google_scholar_key_words(query: str, num_results: 5)
- Best for: academic paper search by keywords
- No API key required
- Slow (scrapes Google Scholar)
- Keep num_results low (5-10)
- Timeout: 25s, retries: 2

## search_google_scholar_advanced(query: str, author: str, year_range: [start, end])
- Best for: advanced academic search with filters
- Timeout: 25s, retries: 2

Rules: Combine with ArXiv for better coverage.
