## search_papers(query: str, max_results: int, categories: ["cs.AI","cs.LG",...], sort_by: "date"|"relevance")
- Best for: technical paper search on ArXiv
- categories filter by CS, physics, math, etc.
- sort_by="date" for latest papers
- Timeout: 25s, retries: 2

## download_paper(paper_id: str)
- Downloads paper content for reading
- Must call BEFORE read_paper
- Timeout: 25s, retries: 2

## read_paper(paper_id: str)
- Reads downloaded paper content
- Requires download_paper first
- Timeout: 25s, retries: 2

Rules: search_papers first, then download, then read.
