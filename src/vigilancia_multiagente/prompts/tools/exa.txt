## web_search_exa(query: str, numResults: 1-100, type: "auto"|"fast"|"deep")
- Best for: general web search with semantic understanding
- Timeout: 20s, retries: 2

## web_search_advanced_exa(query: str, category: "company"|"news"|"people"|"research paper"|"personal site", numResults: 1-100, includeDomains: str[], startPublishedDate: str)
- Best for: company research (category="company"), people (category="people")
- ⚠️ category="company" does NOT accept includeDomains
- ⚠️ includeText only supports single-item arrays
- Timeout: 25s, retries: 2

## web_fetch_exa(url: str)
- Best for: getting full content from a single URL
- Timeout: 20s, retries: 2
