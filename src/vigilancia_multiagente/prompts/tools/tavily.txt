## tavily_search(query: str, search_depth: "basic"|"advanced", max_results: 5-20, include_answer: bool, include_raw_content: bool)
- Best for: general web search, getting quick answers
- Use search_depth="advanced" for deep research
- Set include_answer=True to get a direct AI-generated answer
- Timeout: 20s, retries: 2

## tavily_extract(urls: string[])
- Best for: extracting full content from known URLs
- Use AFTER tavily_search to get detailed page content
- Timeout: 25s, retries: 2

Rules: Start with tavily_search(include_answer=True) for quick context.
Use "advanced" depth only when you need detailed analysis.
