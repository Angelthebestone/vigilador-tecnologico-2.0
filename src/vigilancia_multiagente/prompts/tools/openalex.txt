<tool name="search_works">
  <function_signature>## search_works(query: str, from_year: int, to_year: int, min_citations: int, is_oa: bool, sort: "relevance_score"|"cited_by_count:desc"|"publication_year:desc", per_page: 10-200)</function_signature>
  <best_for>structured bibliometric search: hard data (citation counts, institutions, exact year, concepts) instead of scraped text</best_for>
  <usage>Supports Boolean operators ("machine learning" AND (ethics OR fairness)). Use min_citations=50 for solid papers, 200 for highly influential. Filter by year range for trend windows.</usage>
  <limits>Timeout: 30s, retries: 2</limits>
  <selection_heuristics>
    <prefer_over>Scholar/ArXiv when you need citation counts, institutions or concepts as structured facts (not text)</prefer_over>
    <prefer_over>Web search when mapping the academic landscape of a technology</prefer_over>
    <avoid_when>You need the full paper text (OpenAlex gives metadata + abstract preview only)</avoid_when>
  </selection_heuristics>
  <chaining>
    <step order="1">search_works(query, min_citations=50, sort="cited_by_count:desc") for high-impact works</step>
    <step order="2">get_work(work_id) for full metadata of the most relevant ones</step>
    <step order="3">get_citation_network or get_related_works to map the surrounding research</step>
  </chaining>
  <fallback>If no results, broaden the query or drop min_citations; then try search_papers (ArXiv) or Scholar</fallback>
</tool>
