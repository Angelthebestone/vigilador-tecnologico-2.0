As framed, this is not an investable thesis; it is a narrative wrapper around three weakly-supported data points. The 3–5x revenue opportunity has no derivation, no TAM/SAM/SOM decomposition, and no comparable. A 36-month horizon for a hardware-adjacent business with a 60–80% performance overhead and an unproven regulatory catalyst is too short for a Series C to compound through to a meaningful exit multiple, especially when the exit thesis (strategic acquisition by a regional banking incumbent) lacks any precedent transaction reference. The 0.78 confidence figure carries no derivation procedure, which means it has zero diligence value. Most critically, the report does not articulate a downside, which in practice means the analyst has not stress-tested the position.

The load-bearing assumption is that regulatory pressure materializes within the 36-month window. If the Latin American regulators lag the US by 24+ months — which is the historical base rate for crypto-policy diffusion in the region — the revenue ramp slips outside the fund's investment horizon, and the capital is dead money until a follow-on round at flat terms.
